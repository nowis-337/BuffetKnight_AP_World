# Buffet Knight - Decadent Full Course

## What does randomization do to this game?
The game progresses mostly the same as a normal playthrough with items, skills and upgrades randomised. Only "Insatiable Black Hole" and "More Than You Can Chew" endings are accessible as the end goal of the randomiser. There are some minor tweaks to the end of dungeons and certain quests in randomiser mode.

## What is the goal of Buffet Knight when randomised?
### Default
- Requires the collection of all ingredients and at least achieving the "Insatiable Black Hole" Ending.
### Full Course
- Requires reaching the end of the Casino Bonus Dungeon and achieving the "More Than You Can Chew" Ending.
- Requires the collection of all ingredients, skills and skill upgrades, and all relics.


## What items and locations can get shuffled?

### Locations and Items that are not shuffled:
- Collecting all ingredients to received the "Mysterious Fried Chicken"
- Collecting all relics to received the "Forbidden Insight"
- Receiving the "Gastronomancy Potion" from the "Gastronomy Essence"

### Locations that are shuffled:
- All Treasure chests
- All Remaining Quest locations / rewards
- All Culinary Art Locations
- All Food Skill Locations
- All Food Skill Upgrade Locations
- All Shop Items
- All Blacksmith Upgrades
- All Relic Locations.

### Items / Upgrades that are shuffled:
- Gold
- Gourmet shards and crystals
- Accessories
- Quest items
- Ingredients
- Food skills and upgrades
- Culinary Arts
- Mineral Ores
- Weapon Upgrades
- Armour Upgrades
- Heart Upgrades
- Stomach Upgrades
- Accessory Slot Upgrades
- Relics

If the "Exclude Secret Dungeon" option is selected, no important items will be placed in the secret dream dungeon.


## What does another world's item look like in Buffet Knight?
Items do not change in appearance. A message will appear displaying the item name and the receiving player when picked up.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?
A message in game will notify the item or upgrade received. This is refreshed upon leaving a save point and entering a new map or room.
